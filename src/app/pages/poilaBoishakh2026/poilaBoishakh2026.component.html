About the event

শুভ নববর্ষ — Welcome the New Year

Come and join us to celebrate Poila Boishakh 2026 with your family and enjoy the day with puja and delicious Bengali food.

Poila Boishakh, the first day of the Bengali calendar year, is one of the most joyous festivals celebrated by the Bengali community across the world. The day is marked with traditional rituals, vibrant colors, music, dance and of course — amazing food.

The Northern Virginia Bengali Association (NVBA) warmly invites you and your family to ring in Bengali New Year 1433 together. A day of community, culture and celebration awaits!
