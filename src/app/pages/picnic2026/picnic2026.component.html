About the Event

A Celebration of Community

Join us for NVBA's biggest annual gathering — a day where families come together to enjoy authentic Bengali cuisine, exciting activities, and the warmth of our community.

Kids, adults, and seniors — everyone is welcome to this fun-filled day of adda, games, and delicious bhuri bhoj under the open sky at Bull Run Regional Park.
