About the Event

A Celebration of Bengali Culture

Echoes of Bengal is a vibrant celebration of our rich heritage — bringing together the best of Bengali music, drama, dance, storytelling, and cuisine for an unforgettable day.

From captivating stage performances by our talented community members to mouth-watering food, unique vendors, and a lively book fair, there is something for everyone. Come and celebrate our roots with friends and family!
