About the Event

A Celebration of Bengali Culture

Celebrate the richness of Bengali literature, drama, music & dance! NVBA has always strived to uplift our cultural platform while preserving the richness of Bengali culture.

This year, NVBA is thrilled to bring you for the very first time a full day immersed in Bengali nostalgia, taking you back to your roots as we honor the Bengali maestros in literature, art, music, and drama who have shaped generations.

Join us for an unforgettable cultural experience featuring renowned talents as NVBA brings you Echoes of Bengal (বাংলার প্রতিধ্বনি) — a full‑day festival honoring our shared heritage through storytelling, performance, creativity, and community.
