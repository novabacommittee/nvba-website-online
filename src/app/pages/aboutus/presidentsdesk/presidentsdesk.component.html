From President's Desk

Northern Virginia Bengali Association

A Message From Our President

Arijit Auddy

President, NVBA 2026-27

Dear Members and Friends,

It is both an honor and a privilege to serve as your President of the Northern Virginia Bengali Association. Our association is beyond just an organization — it is the collaboration of a vibrant community that fosters the cultivation of culture, tradition, friendships, and shared identity.

Northern Virginia is home to a diverse Bengali community. Our events and initiatives, ranging from Saraswati Puja and Durga Puja to cultural programs, all promote youth engagement, and community outreach. We strive to celebrate our heritage while fostering unity and belonging for families across generations.

We will continue to preserve and promote the Bengali traditions through language, music, and the arts. We will also make a dedicated effort to inspire the next generation to embrace and carry forward our rich heritage by empowering youth involvement in cultural activities.

In the next two years, NVBA will be focusing on expanding charitable activities by addressing homelessness, food insecurity, and many such local social issues.

The strength of our association lies within its volunteers, sponsors, executive committee members, and the dedicated families who contribute their invaluable time and energy to our initiative. Your support is the catalyst to every successful event that NVBA has brought forth.

As we look ahead to 2026, our vision is to further strengthen NVBA as a leading cultural and community platform in Northern Virginia.

Our Key Focus Areas for 2026-27

  • Community Outreach & Partnerships Building collaborations with local organizations and community groups to expand our impact.
  • Expanded Youth Engagement Launching structured programs for children and young adults, including participation in physical activities such as 5K walk/run events, hiking excursions, and greater involvement in cultural programs. Our goal is to promote leadership, wellness, and a deeper connection to our heritage among the next generation.
  • Enhanced Cultural Excellence Elevating the quality and scale of our signature events while preserving authenticity and inclusivity.
  • Digital & Communication Growth Improving member engagement through enhanced digital presence, streamlined communication, and transparent updates.
  • Volunteer Development Creating structured volunteer pathways to empower more members to take active leadership roles.

Our goal is not only to celebrate traditions but also to create a sustainable and forward-looking organization that adapts to the evolving needs of our community.

I warmly invite all of you to participate and share your ideas. Together, we can continue to build an association that honors our heritage while creating meaningful experiences and memories for generations to come.

Thanks & Regards,

Arijit Auddy

President

Northern Virginia Bengali Association
