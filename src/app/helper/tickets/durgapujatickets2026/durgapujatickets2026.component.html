Event Refund Policy
  1. Event Cancellation by NVBA
    • If an event is cancelled by NVBA, ticket holders will be entitled to 100% refund of the ticket price paid.
  2. Event Rescheduling by NVBA
    • Existing tickets will remain valid for the rescheduled date.
    • Ticket holders who are unable to attend the rescheduled date may request a 100% refund of the ticket price paid.
  3. Ticket Holder-Initiated Cancellation — if a ticket holder voluntarily cancels their attendance and the event proceeds as scheduled:
    • Refund requests must be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the scheduled event date to be eligible for a refund, less any applicable processing fees.
    • Cancellations submitted less than 14 calendar days before the scheduled event date are non-refundable.
    • No refunds will be provided for no-shows.
  4. Pricing Errors or Administrative Issues
    • If a ticket holder is incorrectly charged because of a membership-status error, website or payment-system issue, duplicate charge, or other administrative error, NVBA will refund the incorrectly charged amount.
  5. Weather-Related Cancellations or Rescheduling
    • If NVBA cancels or reschedules an event because of weather, facility closure, safety concerns, or similar circumstances, the refund provisions under Sections 1 or 2 above will apply, as applicable.
  6. All refund requests must be submitted in writing to treasurer@novaba.org. Approved refunds will generally be processed within 4-6 weeks and issued to the original payment method, where reasonably practicable.
